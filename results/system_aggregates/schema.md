# final_matrix_stats schema

Generated by p3x_aggregate.m from the frozen run_matrix.csv (304 rows) and the raw per-run artifacts under runs/<case_id>/seed_<seed>/.

- tidy_runs.csv: one row per (matrix_run_id, seed). Factors are the frozen matrix columns; metrics mirror metrics.json + supplement_metrics.csv + attribution.csv.
- aggregate_ci.csv: long format per (block, case_id, metric); mean/sd/se/95% two-sided t-CI over the 8 common seeds; n_missing counts NaN (missing is NEVER replaced by 0).
- delta_boundary.csv / boundary_report.md: E3 scanned-axis paired deltas vs the common baseline cell E3_W1800 (same seeds = common potential outcomes). crossing_statement is honest: "no_zero_crossing_observed" means zero was never crossed on the scanned levels and NO critical value exists; a crossing yields only a bracket; no interpolation, no densification.
- e1_windows.csv: per-window per-mode descriptive statistics (windows are NOT i.i.d.; cross-window aggregation is descriptive only).
- e2_axes.csv: paired (by seed) contrasts; budget axis at FIXED absolute budget across C (X1-3), capacity axis at fixed budget, demand axis d1 subset of d2.
NaN policy: empty/undefined statistics are written as NaN; count metrics of empty sets are NaN, not 0.
Units: time seconds; carbon = relative carbon index (NOT measured gCO2); quality dimensionless; all scenarios are SUPPLEMENT DEVELOPMENT / MECHANISM CONSTRUCTED.
